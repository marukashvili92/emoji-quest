🎮 Emoji Quest - Data Deletion

We respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. This page explains how you can request deletion of your data from Emoji Quest.

What Data We Store

  • Your display name and profile picture (from Google/Facebook login)
  • Game statistics (wins, losses, draws, trophies)
  • Match history
  • Friends list and chat messages

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: Email Request

Send an email to marukashvili@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: "Data Deletion Request - Emoji Quest"
  • Your registered email address
  • Your username (if known)

We will process your request within 30 days and confirm deletion via email.

Option 2: In-App Deletion

  • Open Emoji Quest app
  • Go to your Profile/Account screen
  • Tap "Logout" to disconnect your account
  • Your local data will be cleared

For complete server-side deletion, please use Option 1.

After Deletion

Once your data is deleted:

  • All your game statistics will be permanently removed
  • Your match history will be deleted
  • Your account will be removed from leaderboards
  • Friends will no longer see your profile

This action is irreversible. You can always create a new account by logging in again.
