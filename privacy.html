🎮 Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 29, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Emoji Quest! This Privacy Policy explains how QuestNest Games ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile game application.

By using Emoji Quest, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your display name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Game Data: Your game statistics, scores, match history, achievements, and progress.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the game, features used, and session duration.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing and maintaining the game service
  • Enabling multiplayer features and friend connections
  • Displaying leaderboards and match history
  • Saving your game progress and preferences
  • Sending notifications about game updates or friend activity
  • Improving game performance and user experience
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring fair play

🔒 Your Privacy Matters: We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Your data is used solely to enhance your gaming experience.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is securely stored using Firebase services provided by Google. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

Game data is stored in the cloud to enable cross-device play and multiplayer features.

Third-Party Services

Emoji Quest uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: For authentication, database, and analytics
  • Google Sign-In: For account authentication
  • Facebook Login: For account authentication

These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Export your data in a portable format

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. Visit our Data Deletion Page for instructions on how to delete your data.

Children's Privacy

Emoji Quest is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
