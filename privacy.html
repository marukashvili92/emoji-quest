Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 29, 2026

Welcome to Tic Tac Toe Emoji Quest, operated by Quest Nest Games. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our application, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: If you log in via Google or Facebook, we receive your name, profile picture, and email address to create your in-game profile.
  • Gameplay Data: We store your wins, losses, trophies, gold, and match history to provide the leaderboard and progression features.
  • Device Information: Basic technical data (OS version, device model) may be collected via Firebase to help us fix bugs and improve performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Maintain your global rank and trophies.
  • Facilitate online matchmaking and social features (friends list, chat).
  • Improve game stability and prevent cheating.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to power our game:

  • Google Firebase: Used for real-time database, authentication, and hosting.
  • Meta (Facebook) Login: Used to provide a seamless social login experience.

These providers have their own privacy policies regarding how they handle your data.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your data as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please contact us at the email below. We will process your request within 7 business days.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
